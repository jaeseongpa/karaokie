Karaokie

Type Thai the way it sounds

Karaokie is an iPhone keyboard. Type Thai in English letters, such as sawasdee krub, and pick the Thai word you meant: สวัสดีครับ.

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard…
  3. Choose Karaokie.
  4. In any app, touch and hold the globe key and choose Karaokie.

Karaokie does not ask for Full Access. You do not need to turn it on.

Common questions

How do I pick a word?
Space picks the first suggestion. Tap another suggestion to pick it instead.

How do I type a word in English?
Start the word with a capital letter, or tap return to keep what you typed as it is.

How do I hide the English meanings?
Tap Meanings on the suggestion bar when you are not typing.

The word I want does not come up.
Try another spelling of the sound, for example khrap, krub or krap. If a common word is missing or wrong, please tell us (below).

Why does the iPhone keyboard appear in some fields?
iOS always uses its own keyboard for passwords and phone numbers.

A meaning looks wrong.
The dictionary and English meanings were written and checked with the help of AI and may contain mistakes. Reports are welcome.

Free trial and Karaokie Plus

What is free?
Typing Thai the way it sounds, numbers, symbols, English, the emoji keyboard and reading Thai messages in the app are free, with no time limit.

What does Karaokie Plus add?
English meanings under suggestions, next-word suggestions and the full list of suggestions for a sound.

How does the free trial work?
Start it in the Karaokie app, from Karaokie Plus on the home screen. It lasts 14 days, ends by itself, charges nothing and does not renew. After it ends, the three Plus features turn off until you buy Karaokie Plus.

Is Karaokie Plus a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase, made in the app through Apple.

I bought Karaokie Plus on another iPhone.
Open the app, tap Karaokie Plus, then Restore Purchases.

Refunds
Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
