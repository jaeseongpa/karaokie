Privacy Policy

Karaokie · Effective 22 November 2026

Karaokie does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What you type

  • The keyboard converts what you type on your iPhone. Nothing you type is saved by Karaokie or sent anywhere.
  • The keyboard does not request Full Access and cannot use the network.
  • Karaokie has no accounts and no sign-in.

What is stored on your device

  • The keyboard remembers one setting: whether English meanings are shown.
  • The app remembers whether you have finished the setup steps.

Both stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

Karaokie contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits.

Purchases

If you choose to leave a tip in the app, the payment is handled by Apple. Karaokie does not receive your payment details and keeps no record of the purchase. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information from Apple

Apple may share aggregated app statistics and crash reports with developers if you have agreed to share them in your iPhone settings. These do not include what you type.

Children

Karaokie does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
