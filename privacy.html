Privacy Policy

Karaokie · Effective 28 November 2026

Karaokie does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What you type

  • The keyboard converts what you type on your iPhone. Nothing you type is saved by Karaokie or sent anywhere.
  • The keyboard does not request Full Access and cannot use the network.
  • Karaokie has no accounts and no sign-in.

What is stored on your device

  • The keyboard remembers one setting: whether English meanings are shown.
  • The app remembers whether you have finished the setup steps and whether it has offered you the free trial.
  • The app saves, in a space on your iPhone that only the app and the keyboard can use, when your free trial ends, whether you bought Karaokie Plus, and when it last updated these two facts. The keyboard only reads them, to know which features to show. Nothing else is shared between the app and the keyboard.
  • When you paste a Thai message into the app to read it, the text is shown on your screen only. It is not saved and not sent anywhere.

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Free trial and purchases

The free trial and Karaokie Plus are in-app purchases sold through Apple. Apple processes them, and Karaokie never receives your payment details. The app uses the network only to reach Apple's App Store for the trial and Karaokie Plus: to show the price, start the trial, buy and restore. The app checks your purchase on your device and does not send it to us or to anyone else. The keyboard never uses the network.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

Karaokie contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits.

Information from Apple

Apple may share aggregated app statistics and crash reports with developers if you have agreed to share them in your iPhone settings. These do not include what you type.

Children

Karaokie does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
